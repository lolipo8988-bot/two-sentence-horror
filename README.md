# Two-Sentence Horror

An entry for the make.fun competition "Two-Sentence Horror": the scariest
story that fits in exactly two sentences (at most 400 characters).

Every night at 3:07, a hallway camera sends the narrator a photo of his
bedroom door, open a fraction wider than the night before. This morning's
photo came from inside the bedroom — the narrator asleep in it, and the
door, behind whoever took it, closed. The second sentence turns the first
into an approach measured night by night, and leaves what is now shut in
the room with him entirely to the reader.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the two-sentence story to `/output/entry.txt` (set `OUTPUT_DIR`
to redirect it for local checks).
